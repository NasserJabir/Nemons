# Research Gaps

This is an initial register of unresolved evidence and product questions.

| Gap | Why it matters | Evidence needed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact primary-source metadata for all recent research leads | Avoid basing decisions on misidentified or incomplete citations | Verify title, authors, date, version, method, limitations | Open |
| Cross-harness context portability | A common format may lose harness-specific meaning | Same tasks across at least two distinct harnesses | Open |
| Memory admission thresholds by category | One threshold may over-retain inference or under-retain decisions | Labeled examples and held-out review | Open |
| Staleness policy by information type | Facts, preferences, and execution observations age differently | Time-based and change-triggered validation tests | Open |
| Retrieval overhead vs benefit | More retrieval can increase noise and latency | Paired tasks with/without retrieval and cost metrics | Open |
| Skill change attribution | Failures may arise outside the Skill | Controlled fault injection and repeated trajectories | Open |
| Shared context under parallel Subagents | Concurrent updates may conflict or duplicate work | Parallel tasks with versioned updates and conflict cases | Open |
| Evidence sufficiency for adoption | No universal threshold is established | Risk-tiered review and regression outcomes | Open |
| Governance authority and separation of duties | Approval policy must be explicit and auditable | Decision record defining roles and change classes | Open |

No numeric thresholds should be treated as accepted until supported by experiments and a decision record.
